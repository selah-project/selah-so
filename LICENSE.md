# Shatiga

**Tarjumaadda Soomaaliga ee Selah** — tarjumaadda mashruuca Selah ee xaraf ahaan daacadda u ah Kitaabka Cibraaniga (tarjumaad aayad kasta iyo micno token kasta), iyada iyo taariikhda dib-u-eegisteeda.

Waxaa lagu shatiyeeyay **Creative Commons Attribution-ShareAlike 4.0 International (CC BY-SA 4.0)**.

<https://creativecommons.org/licenses/by-sa/4.0/>

Waad xor u tahay inaad shaqadan la wadaagto oo aad u habayso ujeeddo kasta, ganacsi ahaan iyo si kaleba, haddii aad si habboon u tixraacdo **mashruuca Selah** (https://selahproject.com), aad muujiso haddii wax laga beddelay, oo aad waxa ka soo baxa ku qaybiso isla shatigan.

Isha Cibraaniga ee hoose waa OpenScriptures Hebrew Bible (WLC), oo dadweynaha u furan (public domain). Tarjumaadda, micnayaasha, calaamadaha iyo taariikhda dib-u-eegistu waa hanti asal ah oo mashruuca Selah leeyahay.
